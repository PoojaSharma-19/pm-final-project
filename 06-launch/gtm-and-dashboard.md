# GTM Launch Plan, Riverty business-unit case

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Feature | Instant Virtual Mastercard & Mobile Wallet |
| Goal | Conversion |
| Launch tier | L, Multi-channel |

## Goal & Audience
- **Goal:** Conversion, Conversion is the right goal because the feature aims to remove the delay between card approval and first use, encouraging customers to replace debit card payments with the Riverty Mastercard. By enabling immediate access through Apple Pay and Google Wallet, the feature directly supports the primary success metric of achieving a 75% Active Card Usage Rate within 30 days while helping Riverty build an everyday customer relationship.
- **Target audience:** Lena – A 32-year-old salaried employee in Cologne with a middling income and little financial cushion who shops primarily on her mobile phone. She prefers to pay only after deciding to keep an item but currently uses her debit card for in-store purchases because Riverty is not immediately available. The Instant Virtual Mastercard enables her to start using Riverty immediately through Apple Pay or Google Wallet, giving her a consistent "Keep it first, pay later" experience across online and physical stores.

## Launch Tier
- **L, Multi-channel**, The Instant Virtual Mastercard is a strategic consumer feature with high adoption and revenue potential. A multi-channel launch will maximise awareness among existing Riverty users, accelerate card activation, and help establish Riverty as an everyday payment option. Remaining silent risks slow adoption, reduced Active Card Usage, and allowing competitors like Klarna to continue building stronger payment habits.

## Channels
1. **Owned: Riverty Mobile App**
2. **Owned: Email Campaign**
3. **Owned: Social Media (Instagram/LinkedIn/Facebook)**

## Enablement & Assets
Product one-pager and sales enablement guide.
Customer support FAQs and wallet setup guide.
In-app banners, email templates, landing page, push notifications, and a short demo video illustrating how to activate and use the Instant Virtual Mastercard through Apple Pay or Google Wallet.

- **Timeline:** Phase 1 – Beta (Friends & Family Pilot)
Duration: Weeks 1–6
Objectives
- Release the Instant Virtual Mastercard to the Friends & Family group.
- Monitor Active Card Usage Rate and BNPL Purchase Success Rate.
- Collect qualitative feedback on onboarding and wallet provisioning.
- Fix critical usability and technical issues.

Phase 2 – Public Launch
Duration: Weeks 7–10
Launch Activities
- Roll out to eligible German Riverty customers.
- Launch multi-channel campaign (App, Email, Social Media).
- Publish landing page and help centre articles.
- Enable customer support with updated documentation.

Phase 3 – Post-Launch Optimisation
Duration: Weeks 11–16
Objectives
- Monitor product performance and customer adoption.
- Analyse user feedback and experiment results.
- Optimise onboarding and wallet provisioning.
- Prioritise the next release (e.g., Return-Aware Refund Matching or Merchant Rewards).

## Success Metrics
- **Metrics:** Active Card Usage Rate: ≥75% of activated customers complete at least one transaction within 30 days.
Wallet Provisioning Success Rate: ≥98% of eligible customers successfully add their card to Apple Pay or Google Wallet.
Time to First Transaction: ≥70% of activated customers make their first purchase within 24 hours of approval.
- **Bad signal to watch for:** BNPL Purchase Success Rate falls below 92%, wallet provisioning success drops below 95%, or customers abandon the onboarding process before completing wallet setup.
- **Likely post-launch decision:** Most Likely Post-Launch Decision - Go: Roll out to all eligible customers if success metrics are achieved and guardrails remain stable. - Iterate: Improve onboarding and wallet provisioning if adoption is below target but customer feedback is positive. - No-Go: Pause rollout if BNPL Purchase Success Rate declines or critical security, provisioning, or payment issues are identified.
