- **The “Now” feature I’m scoping (name + one-line core description):** Instant Virtual Mastercard & Mobile Wallet- Provides immediate access to the card through Apple Pay or Google Pay after approval, encouraging faster activation and everyday usage without waiting for a physical card.
- **My finalized Must-Haves (after overriding the AI):** Instant Virtual Card Generation
Apple Pay & Google Wallet Provisioning
Secure Customer Authentication
Card Activation Confirmation
Real-Time Card Availability
- **What I demoted from Must → Should/Won’t, and why:** Wallet Setup Guide (Must → Should)
Although helpful for first-time users, customers can still successfully add their card using the standard Apple Pay or Google Wallet flow. It improves usability but is not essential for completing the first transaction.
